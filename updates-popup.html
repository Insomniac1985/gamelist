WHAT'S NEW

Account updates

Here’s what changed in game tracking and account setup:

  • Playtime fills in automatically

    When you mark a game Completed or Finished, Gamelist can get its playtime from your linked Steam, PlayStation, or Xbox account. You can change the number yourself or use the reload button to fetch it again.

  • Connect Nintendo from Settings

    Choose Log in with Nintendo, select your account, then copy the link address from Select this person and paste it into Gamelist.

  • Reconnect your platform accounts

    Connect PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, and Nintendo again from Settings. Follow the instructions shown for each platform. Personal account credentials are no longer added as Cloudflare secrets.
