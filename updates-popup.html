WHAT'S NEW

Gamelist updates

Here are the latest changes to Gamelist:

  • Logo navigation

    Tapping the Gamelist logo now scrolls to your games instead of opening Search.

  • Smoother page switching

    Settings stays visible while switching between Gamelist and Shelf when you are signed in. The desktop dock switch button now expands enough to show its full label.

  • Mobile and desktop dock updates

    Switch between Gamelist and Shelf from the mobile dock or the compact desktop dock. On desktop, the dock stays visible, its up arrow appears after scrolling, and the Add and page switch buttons expand to show their bold labels on hover with smoother transitions. Mobile keeps the up arrow separate from the dock. Add Game opens the editor without scrolling to Search, and Shelf settings now match Gamelist with store icons and region flags.

  • New settings page

    The settings page has been revamped to be more slick and accessible.

  • Playtime shows up live on your Currently Playing games!

    A recent update allowed to show your actual playtime when finishing a game using the platforms API, but now it has been updated to also show your "live" playtime right outside on the hard, replacing the approximate total playtime of the game. But there's even more, on hover you will see again the approximate total playtime and see how much you got left.

  • Remember to update to the new Platform account connection system!

    You can now connect IGDB, PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, and Nintendo directly from Settings. No more secrets needed! Follow the instructions shown for each platform.

    They are still as secure, and you probably might need to set them up again using the new system. Feel free to setup and delete the Secrets inside Cloudflare. Or just set it up and leave them there, it's fine. Also this will be very useful for when the PlayStation token expires and innevitably ask you for a new one.
