WHAT'S NEW

Account updates

Game tracking and account connections have been updated:

  • Automatic playtime: when you mark a game completed or finished, Gamelist can fill its time from your linked Steam, PlayStation, or Xbox account. You can still edit the time or refetch it manually.
  • Nintendo account: connect your Nintendo account from Settings using the new sign-in and link-paste flow.
  • Reconnect your accounts: open Settings and connect PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, and Nintendo again. Each platform has its own steps. The old per-account Cloudflare secret setup is no longer used; use the account connection flow in Settings.
